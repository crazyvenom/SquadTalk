# SquadTalk for Android

This app joins the same server and rooms as the PC app. It works on Android 10 and newer, including Android 15 and 16.

## Install

1. On your phone, open **github.com/crazyvenom/SquadTalk/releases** in Chrome.
2. Under **SquadTalk — latest build**, tap **SquadTalk.apk** to download it.
3. Open the downloaded file. Android will say installing from this source isn't allowed yet:
   - Tap **Settings** and turn on **Allow from this source**, then go back.
   - Tap **Install**.
4. If **Play Protect** warns about an unknown app, tap **More details**, then **Install anyway**. This happens because the app isn't from the Play Store.

**Updating:** download the new SquadTalk.apk and install it over the old one. Your settings are kept.

## First join

1. Open SquadTalk and type your name, the **server address**, the **port** (9987) and the **password**.
2. Tap **Join server**. Android asks two things:
   - **Microphone:** tap **While using the app**. This is needed to talk.
   - **Notifications:** tap **Allow**. Android shows a "SquadTalk is running" notification, which keeps voice chat working while you play.

## Ways to talk

| Way | How | Works while in a game? |
|---|---|---|
| Big **Hold to talk** button | Hold it in the app | No, the app has to be on screen |
| **Floating button** | Settings → turn on "Floating talk button over games" → allow **Display over other apps** → hold the 🎙 bubble | **Yes** |
| **Volume Down** | Hold Volume Down | No, the app has to be on screen |
| **Earphone button** | Press once to start talking, press again to stop | Yes, unless the game takes over the button |
| **Voice activation** | Settings → Voice activation, then adjust sensitivity | **Yes** |

Drag the floating bubble to move it out of your game's way.

## Tips

- **Use earphones.** Voice from the phone speaker can be picked up by your own microphone and cause echo.
- **Battery savers:** some phones (Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, Realme, Samsung) stop background apps aggressively. If voice cuts out while gaming, go to **Settings → Apps → SquadTalk → Battery** and choose **Unrestricted** (sometimes called "No restrictions" or "Don't optimise").
- **Leaving:** tap **Leave** in the app, or **Disconnect** in the notification.
